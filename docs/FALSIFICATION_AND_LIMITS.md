# STCM Falsification and Limits

Goal: `STCM-MATHEMATICAL-COMPLETENESS-002`

All candidates remain `candidate_not_proven`. The existing closure harness supplies bounded test evidence only.

## STCM-PC-001 — Conservation-record closure necessity

Falsified under the stated STCM coherence definition if a transition can become a valid receipt basis for a subsequent governed transition while a required conservation field is silently unaccounted and the canonical validators still classify the record as closed/coherent.

## STCM-PC-002 — Finite governed-capacity threshold

Falsified or materially weakened by a reproducible node that sustains `L_g > C_g` indefinitely without any change in load, capacity, routing, refusal, specialization, replication, escalation, phase posture, or coherence quality, while still preserving every governed distinction required by the model.

The current repository does not establish a universal physical measure for `L_g` or `C_g`; domain calibration remains outside this proof candidate.

## STCM-PC-003 — Hash-scoped selective activation

Falsified if a conformant node is activated to semantically evaluate a transition whose relevant hash posture does not match the node's declared scope, absent an explicit separately declared escalation/routing exception.

A platform reading metadata for transport is not automatically node-level governed activation; the counterexample must cross the declared STCM engagement boundary.

## STCM-PC-004 — Non-ignored route existence

Falsified if a canonical governed route step proceeds while one of `hash_match`, `receipt_sufficient`, or `transition_allowed` is false and the route is nevertheless classified as conformant by the model.

## STCM-PC-005 — Selective-activation compute accounting

Falsified if the model's four capacity terms cannot account for bounded transition capacity without overlap/omission under the declared accounting convention, or if strict selective activation systematically requires all nodes to incur the same transition-specific active cost as matched nodes.

The equation is a governance/accounting model, not a physical conservation law and not a claim that hardware energy is literally conserved by these categories.

## STCM-PC-006 — Moment-bound transition validity

Falsified if the model can validly infer a later transition result solely from `P_T(t1)` despite a relevant required/observed value changing at `t2` and without an invariance rule or revalidation step.

## General limits

1. `SATURATED` in PN-001..PN-006 fixtures means the committed bounded fixture set closed under the tested policy; it is not universal mathematical closure.
2. Proof candidates do not create execution, standing, release, publication, certification, AE-admissibility, or master-record authority.
3. Hash-scoped routing is a formal governance rule; deployment/network behavior must be separately verified.
4. Compute accounting categories are model-defined and require domain-specific measurement before quantitative empirical claims.
5. Entropy in STCM is transition-model entropy/unresolved transition potential unless a later artifact explicitly binds it to a physical information-theory quantity.
6. Future Prime Nodes, registry classes, or conservation fields require renewed coverage and falsification testing.
7. The source theory remains `docs/STATE_TRANSITION_CONSERVATION_MODEL.md`; these standard files are adapters for audit and proof maturation, not a competing theory.
