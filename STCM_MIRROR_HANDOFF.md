## Required semantic invariants

1. A receipt may establish evidence for a causal relation but does not manufacture that relation.
2. Two state snapshots or two moment receipts do not establish which states are causally related without an observed/reconstructable causal relation.
3. Finer receipt/observation resolution may expose additional intermediate transitions without erasing a previously established coarse causal transition.
4. Governance admissibility must record the governor/invariant set separately from causal-transition existence.
5. A higher-order restriction on one transition does not automatically taint later trajectory members; lineage contamination requires an explicit lineage invariant.
6. Clock coordinates, epochs, or timestamps are evidentiary unless an explicit governance invariant consumes them as authoritative state.
7. A DENY/FAIL_CLOSED receipt is a governance disposition/response relation, not evidence that no state transition occurred.
